## Notes from Design Meeting

### Overview

The user wants an app to help remember details about friends and family, what they talked about, and what they talked about last time they spoke. The goal is to provide context for reconnecting and nurturing relationships in a more meaningful way. The app should allow the user to log interactions, set goals for the relationship, track key details, and receive reminders to reconnect. The project should be open-source and free to host, with a one-time payment model followed by usage-based pricing for compute/storage.

### Users

- Primary user: The individual who wants to better maintain their social connections.
- Secondary users: Friends and family of the primary user who might be mentioned in entries.
