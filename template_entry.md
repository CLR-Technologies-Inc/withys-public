# Template Entry Example Title

Front matter notes.
Date is a required value. The format of the date is YYYY-MM-DD.
Location is a required value. It is the location of the journal entry, or the system used (email, text, phone, meeting, event, other).

From is an optional value. It is the person the journal entry is from typically used for email.

To is an optional value. It is the person the journal entry is to typically used for email.

Contact is a required value. It is the person the journal entry is about.

With is an optional value. It is the person the journal entry is with typically used for meetings and phone calls, or a comma separated list if multiple people are involved.

## Example Basic Journal Entry

I spoke on the phone with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER

## Example Journal Entry with More Information

I spoke on the phone with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER who is with COMPANY

## Example Journal Entry with All Information

I spoke on the phone with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER who is with COMPANY and the call lasted about TIME

## Example Journal Entry with Tags

I spoke on the phone with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER who is with COMPANY and the call lasted about DATE and we discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4

## Example Journal Entry with Notes

I spoke on the phone with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER who is with COMPANY and the call lasted about DATE and we discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example Email Entry

I received an email from CONTACT with EMAIL_ADDRESS regarding SUBJECT. BODY contained the following critical points: The email discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example Meeting Entry

I met with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER who is with COMPANY and the meeting lasted about DATE and we discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example Event Entry

I attended an event  with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER who is with COMPANY and the event lasted about TIME and we discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example Follow-up Entry

I followed up with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER who is with COMPANY and the follow-up lasted about DATE and we discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example Future Entry

I plan to follow up with CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER who is with COMPANY and the follow-up will last about DATE and we will discuss DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example Text/SMS Entry

I received a text/sms from CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER regarding SUBJECT. The text/sms discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example Voicemail Entry

I received a voicemail from CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER regarding SUBJECT. The voicemail discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example LinkedIn Entry

I received a message from CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER on LinkedIn regarding SUBJECT. The message discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.

## Example Other Entry

I received an entry from CONTACT with PHONE_NUMBER regarding SUBJECT. The entry discussed DATE, TAG1, TAG2, TAG3, and TAG4.
